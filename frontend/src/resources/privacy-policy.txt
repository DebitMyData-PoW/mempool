Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

1. Introduction

At DebitMyData, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any information collected through our web application or associated services.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from users of this application. The app is designed for informational purposes only and does not require account creation, personal data entry, or interaction with private credentials.

However, depending on your browser settings or third-party services you may use (e.g., wallets, block explorers), some non-personally identifiable information may be collected automatically by your browser or operating system.

3. Use of Information

As we do not collect any personal information, there is no user data used, shared, sold, or stored by DebitMyData directly. Any technical data passively collected (e.g., IP address, browser type) is handled by your browser or hosting provider and used only to ensure the app functions properly.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may contain external links to block explorers, exchanges, or related platforms. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of these external sites.

For Android integration, our .well-known/assetlinks.json file is used only to verify secure app-website association. It does not contain any personal data and is safe to be publicly accessible.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies, fingerprinting, or any form of tracking on this site.

6. Security

The app is served over secure HTTPS to ensure your connection is encrypted and tamper-resistant. We do not store or process user data on our servers.
